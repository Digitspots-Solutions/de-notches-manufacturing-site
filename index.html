About Godswill PayTech Solutions

Godswill Digital Payments Limited is a leading fintech company based in Enugu, Nigeria, dedicated to revolutionizing the way Nigerians conduct financial transactions. We provide cutting-edge digital payment solutions that bridge the gap between traditional banking and modern commerce.

Our mission is to create a seamless, secure, and accessible payment ecosystem that empowers businesses of all sizes and individuals to participate fully in Nigeria's growing digital economy. With robust technology infrastructure and a deep understanding of local payment challenges, we deliver solutions that work reliably in the Nigerian context.

Headquartered at No 102 Amechi Road, Awkunanaw in Enugu South, we serve clients across Enugu State and throughout Nigeria, combining international fintech standards with local market expertise.

Digital Payment Solutions

Mobile Money Transfers

Instant peer-to-peer transfers with industry-leading security. Send money across Nigeria quickly and safely using our mobile platform. Zero hidden fees, real-time transaction confirmation, and 24/7 availability.

Merchant Payment Solutions

Accept digital payments from customers with ease. We provide POS terminals, online payment gateways, and integration APIs that work seamlessly with your business operations. Boost sales and improve cash flow management.

Bill Payment Services

Utility bills, airtime, data bundles, cable TV subscriptions, and more. Pay all your bills from one secure platform. Enjoy instant processing and automated receipt generation.

Agent Banking Network

Join our growing network of payment agents across Enugu and Nigeria. Earn commission while providing essential financial services to your community. Full training and technical support provided.

Bulk Payment Processing

Perfect for businesses managing staff salaries, supplier payments, or customer refunds. Process hundreds of transactions simultaneously with enterprise-grade security and detailed reporting.

API Integration Services

Developers and businesses can integrate our payment infrastructure into their platforms. Comprehensive documentation, sandbox environment, and dedicated technical support ensure smooth implementation.
