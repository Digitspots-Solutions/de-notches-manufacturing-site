About Us

De_Notches Manufacturing is a leading manufacturing company based in Enugu South, Nigeria. We pride ourselves on delivering high-quality manufactured products that serve the needs of businesses and individuals across Nigeria. Our state-of-the-art facility at Amechi Road, Awkunanaw combines modern technology with skilled craftsmanship to ensure every product meets rigorous quality standards. With years of experience in the manufacturing sector, we understand the unique needs of the Nigerian market and are committed to providing reliable, durable, and cost-effective manufacturing solutions. Our team of dedicated professionals works tirelessly to maintain our reputation as a trusted manufacturing partner in Enugu and beyond.
